# Benchmark: Apple TV+ on connected TV, desktop and mobile

Resolves [issue 5](https://github.com/robdhyman/fire-tv/issues/5). Researched 2 Nov 2026 against the Apple Human Interface Guidelines (tvOS), Apple developer documentation (UIKit focus, TVUIKit, AVKit, TVServices), WWDC and Apple TV Tech Talk sessions, the Apple TV App User Guide and Apple TV 4K User Guide on support.apple.com, Apple Newsroom, and trade-press teardowns. Vocabulary follows `GLOSSARY.md` (hero, row, tile, focus, Up Next, device class).

**Naming.** Apple's own terms drift, so this doc maps them: Apple's "Up Next" queue was renamed **Continue Watching** and split from a new **Watchlist** row in tvOS/iOS 18.1 (Sept–Oct 2024); the service "Apple TV+" became **Apple TV** on 13 Oct 2025, so "Apple TV" now names the hardware, the aggregator app and the subscription. Apple's "shelf" and "lockup" are our row and tile. Apple's end-of-episode card is a **content proposal**; ours is the Up Next countdown. Apple's "Top Shelf" is the tvOS home-screen preview strip above the Dock, not part of the app.

**Generations.** G1: tabbed app (Watch Now / TV+ / Store / Search) 2019–Dec 2023, with an autoplaying featured hero added server-side Nov 2022. G2: sidebar redesign, tvOS 17.2, 11 Dec 2023; Watch Now renamed Home. G3: tvOS 18.1 onward (Continue Watching + Watchlist; InSight; automatic subtitles), then tvOS 26 (15 Sept 2025) with Liquid Glass chrome, floating player controls and portrait 2:3 tiles replacing 16:9. Support pages were fetched live and describe G3 unless noted.

## Summary

Apple TV+ on connected TV is the clearest statement of the "focus engine" idiom: one spatial focus at a time, the focused tile lifts, grows about 9–14 percent, gains a specular sheen and tilts with the thumb, never moves without the viewer's input, and every other element dims after inactivity. Navigation is a left sidebar (Search, Home, Apple TV, Store, Library, channels) summoned with Back or a swipe, over a Home of a featured hero carousel, Continue Watching, Watchlist, charts and recommendation rows. The hero autoplays video with sound after about one second of dwell, cannot be hidden, and is the app's most criticised feature. The player uses the tvOS 15 system transport bar: thin timeline, title above, controls on the trailing side; left/right skips 10 s, holding cycles 2x/3x/4x, pausing then swiping scrubs with a thumbnail, swipe down opens Info / InSight / Chapters / Continue Watching tabs, swipe up opens Subtitles and Audio Options. Skip Intro and Skip Recap are "contextual actions" shown bottom-trailing only while the transport bar is hidden; the next-episode card is a content proposal that shrinks the video and auto-accepts on a timer. Skipping back 10 s turns subtitles on for 30 s, up to three times; muting does the same. On third-party TVs including Fire TV, the app re-implements accessibility settings in-app (bold text, text size, high-contrast focus style, reduce motion, caption styles, AD, sign language) because it cannot rely on the host OS, while on tvOS and iOS those live in system Settings. Desktop (Mac app and tv.apple.com) swaps focus for hover-revealed Play and More buttons and a pointer-driven player; mobile swaps it for tap and touch-and-hold, adds picture-in-picture, downloads and playback speed, and on Android drops Home, Store and Library altogether.

## Detailed findings

### Home and hero

- Sidebar (G2+): Search, Home, Apple TV, MLS, Formula 1, Store, Library, then subscribed channels and apps; profile selector at the top; open with Back/Menu or swipe up, "you may have to press or swipe multiple times"; close by swiping right. Press-and-hold a channel to Pin or Hide. [Navigate the Apple TV app (tvOS)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/navigate-the-apple-tv-app-atvb59b423ba/tvos). On generic remotes "press the back, up, or left button"; the profile switch "refreshes with the new user's watchlist, video collections, and recommendations". [Navigate (all devices)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/navigate-the-apple-tv-app-atvb5e608b5e/web).
- Home rows: "Featured items appear at the top of the screen. You press the Next button to move through them and press left on the remote to go back", then Continue Watching, then "top charts, new releases, curated collections, and personalized recommendations"; selecting a row title opens the category. [Find things to watch (tvOS)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/find-things-to-watch-atvb7d7bec9f/tvos).
- The 2023 redesign announcement: "new sidebar allows users to quickly navigate the app"; Home is "a unified guide" with New Shows & Movies, Top Charts, Trending, For You and a Channels & Apps shelf; Store unifies movies and TV shows; profiles are a living-room feature. [Apple Newsroom, 11 Dec 2023](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/12/redesigned-apple-tv-app-elevates-the-viewing-experience/). 17.4 added the autoplay toggle; 17.5 added pinning. [What's new in tvOS 17](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/atvb457e13ae/17.0/tvos).
- Hero autoplay: a Featured section placed above Up Next in Nov 2022, "the featured content seems to autoplay"; "no way to disable the featured section". [MacRumors, 3 Nov 2022](https://www.macrumors.com/2022/11/03/tv-app-redesign-featured/). Resting focus "for about one second" starts video and audio; Settings > Apps > TV > Autoplay Video Sound mutes it but "doesn't stop video previews from automatically playing". [9to5Mac, 10 Jan 2023](https://9to5mac.com/2023/01/10/apple-tv-app-watch-now-auto-play/). tvOS 18.4 added "a large plus button" and "a big 'i' button" to the carousel lockup. [9to5Mac, 31 Mar 2025](https://9to5mac.com/2025/03/31/tvos-18-4-now-available-with-new-design-changes-for-apple-tv-app/). In Mar 2026 autoplaying previews spread to lower rows: "it only autoplays if you stop scrolling for long enough". [9to5Mac, 19 Mar 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/19/apples-tv-app-just-added-autoplaying-video-to-more-parts-of-the-app).
- Apple's own guidance for the equivalent system idiom (Top Shelf): "Provide three to eight images", auto-advance "on a preset timer until someone focuses one"; "Avoid promoting content people have already purchased, rented, or watched"; "Avoid advertisements or prices." [HIG Top Shelf](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/top-shelf). Carousel videos "1080p... around 2 to 5 minutes", "between 5 and 10" items; audio "only plays... on explicit user action"; "Every swipe or click on the remote, it adds to somebody's cognitive load." [WWDC19 211](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2019/211/).
- Criticism: Up Next pushed "off of the screen", For You "the 12th row down", "The Apple TV in my living room isn't Apple's electronic billboard." [Six Colors, Nov 2022](https://sixcolors.com/post/2022/11/up-next-disappointment/). Of the sidebar: "None of the core functions can be moved around or hidden"; "16 swipes down to get to that personalized 'For You' row". [Joe Steel, 11 Dec 2023](https://joe-steel.com/2023-12-11-Underbaked-tvOS-172.html).

### Rows, tiles and focus

- Focus model: "selection requires a separate gesture"; five states (unfocused, focused, highlighted, selected, unavailable); the focused item "stands out... through elevation to the foreground, illumination, and animation"; "Avoid displaying a pointer"; "Make every element reachable"; "Avoid changing focus without people's interaction"; in full-screen media "let people use gestures to interact with the content, not to move focus." [HIG Focus and selection](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/focus-and-selection).
- Focus engine: "the next focusable item in the direction in which the user is navigating becomes focused"; "Only a single item can have focus"; "there's no API for directly setting the focused item"; tab bars auto-select "after a slight delay". [About focus interactions for Apple TV](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/about-focus-interactions-for-apple-tv). Initial focus defaults to the top-left focusable view; "a harder or longer swipe can pass through several views"; invisible focus guides bridge gaps. [Tech Talks 2015, Focus-Driven Interfaces](https://nonstrict.eu/wwdcindex/tech-talks/2015-apple-tv-3/) (mirror). "The Focus engine adds a high-contrast ring for accessibility." [WWDC16 215](https://nonstrict.eu/wwdcindex/wwdc2016/215/) (mirror).
- Tile (lockup) behaviour: "All three views expand and contract together as the lockup gets focus"; poster title and subtitle "are hidden until the poster comes into focus"; caption buttons "tilt with the motion that they swipe"; "A focused lockup expands in size, so leave enough room between lockups." [HIG Lockups](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/lockups). TVLockupView: "Views don't move as other views come into focus." [TVLockupView](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/tvuikit/tvlockupview).
- Sizes: poster 2:3 unfocused 333x570 pt, focused 380x570 (+14% width); square 500 to 570 (+14%); 16:9 782x440 to 852x479 (+9%). [HIG Top Shelf](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/top-shelf). Grid: 5 columns at 320 pt wide, 40 pt gap, 100 pt vertical; 6 columns 260 pt; safe inset 60 pt top/bottom, 80 pt sides (WWDC19 211 says 90). [HIG Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout). Type: body 29 pt, minimum 23 pt, Title 1 76 pt; controls default 66x66 pt, minimum 56x56. [HIG Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography), [HIG Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility).
- Parallax: layered images of "two to five distinct layers", "designed to be almost unnoticeable"; on focus "the system elevates it to the foreground, gently swaying it while applying illumination"; "After a period of inactivity, out-of-focus content dims and the focused element expands." [HIG Images](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/images). Imitating the system parallax "similar but slightly off... is likely to distract people." [Tech Talks 2015, Designing for Apple TV](https://nonstrict.eu/wwdcindex/tech-talks/2015-apple-tv-2/) (mirror). Since tvOS 26 a focused image "renders... with a Liquid Glass effect". [adjustsImageWhenAncestorFocused](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiimageview/adjustsimagewhenancestorfocused).
- tvOS 26 tiles: "new vertical poster art replacing the old artwork design", "Content density has improved quite a bit". [9to5Mac, 16 Jul 2025](https://9to5mac.com/?p=1010274).
- Desktop: "Hovering over a Continue Watching thumbnail reveals a Play button that resumes where you left off"; hover any thumbnail for More (Add/Remove from Watchlist, Share). [Mac guide, start watching](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/tvapp-mac/atv7bf64f99/mac). Mobile: "touch and hold the item, then tap Add to Watchlist". [Find things to watch (all devices)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/find-things-to-watch-atvbb35c9fc3/web).

### Show page and season/episode model

- Selecting a show opens "a new screen [that] appears with ratings, descriptions, and viewing information"; buttons Play, Subscribe, Buy, Open In, Add to Up Next ("In Up Next" once added); "Select an episode, or select a season, then browse episodes"; How to Watch, Related, Cast & Crew rows; details at the bottom. [Apple TV guide, tvOS 15](https://support.apple.com/en-gu/guide/tv/atvb314f957c/15.0/tvos) (G1). Current wording: play, buy/rent/preorder, Add (becomes a tick; selecting the tick removes); "scroll down to view specific episodes, trailers, related items, and additional details". [Find things to watch (tvOS)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/find-things-to-watch-atvb7d7bec9f/tvos).
- Title pages carry AD, CC and SDH badges; on TVs "scroll to the Languages and Accessibility sections". [Apple support 118409](https://support.apple.com/118409).
- Season picker: described by a user as "a season selector, to jump to episode 1 of the selected season above a horizontal row of episodes". [Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/254987025) (unverified; no Apple page describes the control). Button copy such as "Resume S1 E3" and progress bars on episode stills: not documented in any source fetched.

### Player chrome and scrubbing

- tvOS 15 player: "we entirely redesigned the standard playback UI"; "avoid adding extra gestures to the player". [WWDC21 10191](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2021/10191/). Title view above the transport bar; the bar "displays controls along its trailing side"; system adds subtitle and PiP controls; Info tab "may show up to two UIAction controls" (default: play from beginning); Chapters tab appears when markers are set. [Customizing the tvOS playback experience](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avkit/customizing-the-tvos-playback-experience).
- Controls: "Rest your finger on the clickpad... or press up on the clickpad ring" to show, Back to hide; left/right skip 10 s; hold for continuous, "Press repeatedly to cycle through... (2x, 3x, 4x)"; pause then swipe to scrub, "A preview thumbnail appears above the timeline. For finer control, circle your finger around the clickpad ring"; Play/Pause commits, Back cancels; swipe down for Info, InSight, Chapters, Continue Watching; swipe up for Subtitles (with Style / Manage Styles), Audio Options (Enhance Dialogue: Enhance More / Enhance / Off, Reduce Loud Sounds, Audio Track) and PiP. [Control video playback (tvOS)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/control-video-playback-atvb7944597f/tvos). Generic remotes: centre = play/pause; "Skipping backward turns on subtitles automatically"; hide controls "with back button or wait"; PiP "Available on mobile devices or computers". [Control playback (all devices)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/control-playback-atvb5e386e66/web).
- HIG player rules: "Use the system video player"; "Avoid asking people if they want to resume playback"; "Show the expected content immediately"; loading over 2 s gets a black screen with a centred spinner; interactive overlays wait "a minimum delay of 0.5 seconds" before pausing; "prefer translucent SDR graphics, because some devices are prone to image retention." [HIG Playing video](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video). Swipe-up "is reserved by AVPlayerViewController" since tvOS 13. [customOverlayViewController](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avkit/avplayerviewcontroller/customoverlayviewcontroller).
- InSight (tvOS 18): "timely information about actors, characters, and music" during Apple TV+ titles; also on an iPhone used as a remote. [Apple Newsroom, 10 Jun 2024](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/06/updates-to-the-home-experience-elevate-entertainment-and-bring-more-convenience/). tvOS 26 player: "Liquid Glass controls float above your video without obstructing what's happening underneath." [9to5Mac, 16 Jul 2025](https://9to5mac.com/?p=1010274).
- Siri: "What did she just say?" "skips back 10 seconds and temporarily turns on subtitles"; also "Skip forward 90 seconds", "Jump back 10 minutes", "Turn on French subtitles", "Play from the beginning". [Control playback with Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/control-playback-with-siri-atvbd4af2c21/tvos).
- Desktop: pointer reveals controls; Skip Back/Forward 10 s; click timeline or drag playhead; Subtitles and Audio Options lower right; PiP upper left with a resizable viewer; Mute key turns on subtitles. [Mac guide, control playback](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/tvapp-mac/atv31d6caa7/mac). Shortcuts: Space, Cmd-Up/Down, Ctrl-Cmd-F, Cmd-I. [Mac keyboard shortcuts](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/tvapp-mac/atvc572afcf3/mac). Web: "up to 4K HDR" in Safari on Mac, "up to 1080p" in Chrome, Firefox or Edge. [Apple support HT207949](https://support.apple.com/HT207949). Web player chrome itself: not documented by Apple.
- Mobile: tap for controls; "Select a playback speed option on iPhone or iPad"; Enhance Dialogue iPhone/iPad only; PiP by button; Cast on Android. [Control playback (all devices)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/control-playback-atvb5e386e66/web), [Picture in Picture](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/turn-on-picture-in-picture-viewing-atvb71d09d11/web).

### Skip intro/credits and next-episode countdown

- Skip Intro / Skip Recap are AVKit "contextual actions": "present action controls for a specific time in the presentation, such as showing a Skip Intro button during a title sequence"; "The player displays them along the bottom-trailing side of the screen"; "The view controller presents contextual actions only when the transport bar isn't visible"; dismissed by setting the array empty. [contextualActions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avkit/avplayerviewcontroller/contextualactions). WWDC21 notes they "maintain the Now Playing status" and behave as normal focus targets. [WWDC21 10191](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2021/10191/). Apple does not publish the TV+ app's own skip timings; a Chrome extension exists to auto-click them on tv.apple.com, confirming they appear there. [Apple TV Skipper](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/mkcpablaampjifhidkcelogaijhemoef).
- Interstitials: ad or legal ranges appear "as small dots on the player's timeline" and are collapsed from the time display; `requiresLinearPlayback` blocks skipping. [Working with interstitial content](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avkit/working-with-interstitial-content).
- Next episode: an `AVContentProposal` shown "over the currently playing full-screen video", with the example shrinking the video to 960x540, presenting 10 s before the end, and `automaticAcceptanceInterval` auto-accepting; WWDC16 demos used 15 s and 30 s. [Presenting content proposals in tvOS](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avkit/presenting-content-proposals-in-tvos), [AVContentProposal](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avkit/avcontentproposal). Toggles: Play Next Episode, Play a Recommendation, Autoplay Video Sound. [Control autoplay](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/control-autoplay-for-videos-and-sound-atvbd0b95e5d/web). A pre-show trailer with Skip and Add buttons appeared in 17.2 betas. [9to5Mac, 16 Nov 2023](https://9to5mac.com/2023/11/16/tvos-apple-tv-add-to-queue-trailer-button/). The TV+ countdown length is not documented.

### Up Next, Continue Watching and Watchlist

- Continue Watching holds "items you've added to your watchlist or started but haven't finished" and live sports from subscribed channels; selecting one "immediately pick[s] up where you left off". Watchlist holds explicitly added titles; "Movies and TV shows you add appear in the Continue Watching and Watchlist rows." Add or remove by press-and-hold. [Find things to watch (tvOS)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/find-things-to-watch-atvb7d7bec9f/tvos). Clear Play History "also removes TV shows and movies from your Continue Watching and Watchlist rows". [Privacy settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/adjust-privacy-settings-atvb2eb91635/web).
- Rename: "Apple has renamed the Up Next queue in the TV app as Continue Watching"; the new Watchlist row sits far down Home. [iDownloadBlog, 25 Sep 2024](https://www.idownloadblog.com/2024/09/25/ios-18-1-apple-tv-watchlist-section/). Players' end card changed "from 'Up Next' to 'Continue Watching'". [Gadget Hacks](https://apple.gadgethacks.com/how-to/apples-big-tv-app-update-gives-you-10-new-features-and-changes-enhance-your-viewing-experience-0385626).
- Top Shelf: Settings > Apps > TV chooses "What to Watch" previews or "Continue Watching to see what's next in your personal queue". [Adjust TV app settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/adjust-tv-app-settings-atvbae11a68b/tvos). iOS auto-downloads "next two episodes of shows in Continue Watching". [iPhone and iPad settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/iphone-and-ipad-settings-atvb56f341b4/web).
- Ordering rules and when a finished show leaves Continue Watching: not documented.

### Search and on-screen keyboard

- Search finds "by title, cast, genre, and more", offers browsable categories before typing, and accepts natural language ("popular dramas with a twist"); Genius Browse is TV-only. [Find things to watch (all devices)](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/find-things-to-watch-atvbb35c9fc3/web).
- Keyboard: "tvOS displays a linear virtual keyboard when people select a text field using the Siri Remote"; "A grid keyboard screen appears when people use devices other than the Siri Remote." [HIG Virtual keyboards](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/virtual-keyboards). Play/Pause cycles lower/upper/symbols; hold a key for accents. [Enter text](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/enter-text-atvb2ae48ba6/tvos). "People typically don't want to do a lot of typing in tvOS... provide popular and context-specific search suggestions, including recent searches." [HIG Search fields](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/search-fields). Dictation by holding Siri with a field focused; an iPhone notification offers itself as a keyboard. [Dictation](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/activate-siri-search-or-dictation-atvb16053d68/tvos), [iPhone as keyboard](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/use-your-iphone-or-ipad-as-a-keyboard-atvb827791d6/tvos).
- Sign-in on Fire TV and other TVs: "Sign in on mobile device" via QR or link.apple.com code; "Sign in with remote" only on some devices; on US Fire TV "you can't buy other content in the app". [Apple support HT209440](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT209440).

### Captions, audio description and accessibility

- tvOS: Settings > Accessibility > Subtitles and Captioning > Closed Captions and SDH; Style > Edit Styles: "Font, size, and color / Background color and opacity / Text opacity, edge style, and highlight". [Turn on CC and SDH](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/turn-closed-captions-sdh-apple-tv-4k-atvb1d61ca06/tvos). Automatic Subtitles: Show for Different Languages, Show When Muted, Show on Skip Back ("up to 30 seconds"). [Change subtitle settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/change-subtitle-settings-atvb7d97de8b/tvos). AD via Audio Options or Settings; with VoiceOver on, triple-press centre toggles AD, otherwise captions. [Use audio descriptions](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/use-audio-descriptions-atvb372a82a4/tvos).
- Third-party TVs: the app's own Settings > Accessibility holds Bold Text, Text Size, Reduce Transparency, Focus Style "High Contrast", Reduce Motion ("Stops content from autoplaying or auto-scrolling"), Auto-Play Video Previews, Audio Descriptions, Closed Captions and SDH with styles Transparent Background, Large Text, Classic, Outline Text plus Edit Styles, and Sign Language; "captioning may be disabled on devices with built-in captioning." [Change accessibility settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/change-accessibility-settings-atvbaeff85db/web). Screen reading uses "the built-in screen reader on your smart TV"; directional buttons announce items and "End" at edges. [Use screen readers](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp/use-screen-readers-atvbfa4ff6cd/web).
- tvOS accessibility set: VoiceOver (Navigation and Exploration modes, rotor by rotating two fingers), Zoom "up to 15 times", Hover Text, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion, Switch Control, Tap to Navigate, Accessibility Shortcut by pressing Back three times. [Accessibility at a glance](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/accessibility-features-at-a-glance-atvbaeff85db/tvos), [VoiceOver](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/use-voiceover-atvbfa4ff6cd/tvos), [Accessibility Shortcut](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/add-an-accessibility-shortcut-atvb0a315d10/tvos).
- HIG: Reduce Motion means "reducing automatic and repetitive animations, including zooming, scaling, and peripheral motion" and "Replacing transitions in x-, y-, and z-axes with fades"; contrast 4.5:1 under 18 pt; "Avoid autoplaying audio and video content without also providing controls." [HIG Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility).
- AD coverage: "Every original show comes with audio description." [Henshaws](https://www.henshaws.org.uk/hints-and-tips/tv-streaming-services-how-accessible-are-they/). "The availability of accessibility features varies by country and region." [Apple support 118409](https://support.apple.com/118409). Criticism: VoiceOver in the iOS search tab "just says button". [AppleVis](https://applevis.com/comment/135015).

### Motion and transition idiom

- "Embrace the tvOS focus system. Let it gently highlight and expand onscreen items"; "edge-to-edge artwork, subtle and fluid animations"; viewers sit "often 8 feet or more" away. [HIG Designing for tvOS](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-tvos).
- Timing: "when focus is moving quickly, the timing of the animations are sped up"; "focusing animations typically run faster than unfocusing animations, to create a trail effect"; "never assume a fixed duration". [UIFocusAnimationCoordinator](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uifocusanimationcoordinator). Focus sounds: "very large focus items have a larger, deeper sound and smaller items have a smaller, higher pitched sound", quieter when fast. [WWDC17 224](https://nonstrict.eu/wwdcindex/wwdc2017/224/) (mirror). Highlighted state is a brief press-in. [Tech Talks 2015, Designing for Apple TV](https://nonstrict.eu/wwdcindex/tech-talks/2015-apple-tv-2/) (mirror).
- Swipe physics: a swipe "starts fast and then slows down, based on the strength of the swipe"; edge swipes "speed through items very quickly"; "people press before swiping to activate scrubbing mode"; resting a thumb "shows people where to swipe down". [HIG Remotes](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/remotes). "Don't add motion for the sake of adding motion"; "Make motion optional". [HIG Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion).
- Handoff into playback: "The TV app fades to black... Immediately present your own black screen." [HIG Playing video](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video). The Home-to-show-page transition is not described in any Apple source.

## Worth copying for the FireTV app

- One spatial focus, never moved by the app: focus only follows the D-pad, initial focus is top-left, and a focused tile grows about 10 percent, lifts and brightens while neighbours stay put, so a row never reflows.
- Focus animation speeds up with input rate and unfocus lags focus, giving a trail when the viewer skims a row; dim unfocused content after inactivity.
- Poster title and logline hidden until focus; the hero lockup is Play, `+` and `i` in that order.
- Skip-back-turns-on-subtitles (10 s skip, captions for 30 s, up to three times) and mute-shows-subtitles: cheap, loved, and ours to add to Tools as an "Automatic captions" toggle.
- Skip Intro / Skip Recap appear bottom-right only while the player chrome is hidden, as focusable buttons, and vanish when the chrome shows; the Up Next card shrinks the picture rather than covering it and auto-accepts on a visible timer with a Play Next Episode toggle in Tools.
- Scrub model for a D-pad: left/right = 10 s, hold = 2x/3x/4x, pause then left/right to walk the timeline with a thumbnail, Select commits, Back cancels to the original position.
- Re-implement accessibility inside the app on Fire TV (text size, high-contrast focus style, reduce motion, caption styles, AD) rather than deferring to the host, since Apple does exactly this on third-party TVs.
- Search offers categories and recent searches before any typing; keep text entry minimal and accept Alexa voice.
- Layout numbers: 60 pt top/bottom and 80 pt side safe inset at 1920x1080, 40 pt tile gap, body 29 pt and nothing under 23 pt, controls at least 56 pt.

## Worth avoiding

- Autoplaying hero video with sound after one second of dwell, with no way to turn the video off: the single most criticised Apple TV decision across four years of coverage. Default to still art, preview only on a longer dwell, and give Tools a switch.
- Pushing Up Next and personal rows below a promotional hero; keep Up Next first.
- A fixed sidebar whose entries cannot be reordered or hidden, and that needs "multiple" presses to open on a Fire TV remote; our Fire TV nav should open on one Back press or sit in a top bar.
- Imitating the parallax sheen or Liquid Glass approximately; Apple warns "similar but slightly off" distracts, so pick a flat scale-and-glow focus and do it crisply.
- Splitting "Up Next" into two rows with overlapping membership (Continue Watching vs Watchlist); keep one Up Next row and one Watchlist screen as the glossary defines.
- A mobile build that drops core screens (Android lacks Home, Store and Library); the three layouts should share one structure.
- Trailer pre-roll before an episode (17.2 beta Skip/Add interstitial).

## Open questions

- Exact TV+ timings for Skip Intro visibility and the next-episode countdown are not published by Apple; measure on a device before fixing our 15 s.
- The season selector control type (pills vs dropdown) and episode progress bars are not documented; a screenshot pass on Fire TV is needed.
- Fire TV remote mapping inside the Apple TV app (Menu button, long-press behaviour, Alexa voice search) has no official source from Apple or Amazon.
- tv.apple.com player chrome, keyboard shortcuts and hover behaviour are undocumented; only resolution limits are published.
- Safe-zone side inset is 80 pt in the HIG and 90 pt in WWDC19 211; pick one for the style guide.
