# SYFY and USA typefaces: originals and open substitutes

Resolves [#4](https://github.com/robdhyman/fire-tv/issues/4). Researched 2026-11-02.

## Summary

- **SYFY display** was **SYFY Hero**, a proprietary narrow, bold, octagonal headline face drawn by loyalkaspar for the June 2017 rebrand and finished by Dyana Weissman (Font Bureau / Type Network). Confidence: high; three independent sources plus the screenshots agree.
- **USA display** was a **wide geometric grotesk in the Gotham / Core Sans A class**, set in caps. USA ran Gotham from 2005, switched to ITC Avant Garde Gothic in a 2013 refresh, and by the 2016 "We the Bold" refresh press described its type as resembling Clobber Grotesk Light / Core Sans AR 45. The app screenshots (vertical-sided M, straight-leg R, round O) fit the Gotham class, not Avant Garde. Confidence: medium; no source names the exact cut used in the app.
- **Shared UI sans** on both apps is the same unnamed geometric-humanist sans (light nav, regular loglines, bold `E<n>` labels; single-storey g, double-storey a). It is not SYFY Sidekick (Sidekick is blocky; the UI face has round bowls) and is most likely a Proxima Nova / Gotham-class face from NBCU's shared app template. Confidence: medium-low.

| Role | Original (as identified) | Google Fonts substitute | Weights | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SYFY display, condensed bold | SYFY Hero (custom, loyalkaspar + Font Bureau, 2017) | **Barlow Condensed** | 700 (titles), 800 (hero) | OFL 1.1 |
| USA display, wide geometric | Gotham-class geometric grotesk (custom/licensed, unnamed) | **Montserrat** | 700 (titles), 600 (episode names) | OFL 1.1 |
| Shared UI sans | Unnamed geometric-humanist sans, Proxima Nova class | **Figtree** | 300 (nav), 400 (body), 600 (labels, `E<n>`) | OFL 1.1 |

## Findings

### SYFY (2017 rebrand, loyalkaspar)

1. The identity launched on air 19 June 2017 with two custom typefaces, SYFY Hero and SYFY Sidekick, drawn by loyalkaspar and produced with Font Bureau; loyalkaspar also had Grilli Type customise GT Cinetype for the system. Source: [Fonts In Use, "SYFY (2017–)"](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/21272/syfy-2017), contributed by loyalkaspar; formats tagged Web, Advertising, Film/Video, Mobile/Tablet.
2. Type Network's case study describes Hero as a "narrow headline face" whose forms echo the logo's blocky letters and 45-degree angles, for headlines, "followed by SYFY Sidekick for secondary headlines and short paragraphs." Dyana Weissman engineered both into finished fonts with roman and oblique styles; Cyrus Highsmith consulted. Source: [Type Network, "Font Bureau helps loyalkaspar take type to uncharted territories for SYFY"](https://typenetwork.com/articles/font-bureau-helps-loyalkaspar-take-type-to-uncharted-territories-for-syfy).
3. It's Nice That shows the specimens: Hero Bold is a bold condensed sans; Sidekick Regular is "blocky, geometric". ECD Daniel Doernemann: the faces nod to headline fonts from WIRED and The New York Times. Source: [It's Nice That, 27 June 2017](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/loyalkaspar-syfy-channel-rebrand-graphic-design-270617).
4. loyalkaspar's own case study confirms two custom faces for a "cross-platform editorial system" spanning linear, digital, social and experiential, but names no roles. Source: [loyalkaspar, SYFY](https://loyalkaspar.squarespace.com/syfy); the agency's [custom fonts page](https://www.loyalkaspar.com/custom-fonts) lists "HERO & SIDEKICK" with Type Network as partner.
5. Luc Devroye's index lists "SYFY Hero (an octagonal typeface; for NBC Universal)" under loyalkaspar. Source: [luc.devroye.org](https://luc.devroye.org/fonts-104336.html).
6. GT Cinetype (Grilli Type, 2015, Paolozzi and Koch) is a curve-free sans derived from a laser subtitling machine; Light/Regular/Bold plus Mono, commercial licence. It is plausibly the on-air caption face; nothing in the app screenshots is set in it. Source: [Grilli Type, GT Cinetype](https://www.grillitype.com/typeface/gt-cinetype).
7. Screenshots (`nbcu-firetv-assets/`, show page for The Magicians, shows grid): the show title is unmistakably Hero (angled cut terminals on C, G and S; flat-sided bowls). Nav, logline, season tabs and `E<n>` labels are a different, curved sans; the same face appears pixel-for-pixel in the USA screenshots.

### USA Network (2016 "We the Bold" refresh)

1. The 2016 refresh was a tagline and positioning change, not a visual overhaul. BBH Global wrote the line; Lee Hunt advised; USA said it had "no immediate plans for a larger rebrand." The tagline was set "in different fonts and visuals" inside each show's world, so there is no single 2016 tagline face. Sources: [Promax, 14 April 2016](https://oldbrief.promax.org/article/usa-network-revamps-brand-with-we-the-bold-tagline); [Promax, 10 May 2016](https://oldbrief.promax.org/article/why-usa-rebranded-to-reflect-a-risk-taking-american-spirit); [Comcast press release](https://corporate.comcast.com/news-information/news-feed/we-the-bold-usa-network-launches-new-brand-positioning).
2. The last typographic change before that was the September 2013 refresh with Leroy + Clarkson, which changed "its brand font to Avant Garde from Gotham" and introduced cross-screen "screen unification." Source: [Promax, 27 September 2013](https://oldbrief.promax.org/article/usa-network-refreshes-brand-with-premieres-of-modern-family-ncis-la).
3. NewscastStudio's November 2018 coverage of the next refresh says USA kept its existing typographic look, "a font similar to Clobber Grotesk Light or Core Sans AR 45." Source: [NewscastStudio, 27 November 2018](https://www.newscaststudio.com/2018/11/27/usa-network-branding/) (quoted from the search excerpt; the page refused automated fetches).
4. loyalkaspar later rebranded USA too, and for the 2018 Harry Potter package derived "Flagrate" from SYFY Hero so it could "live on USA without feeling like a SYFY takeover." This is after our 2017 window. Source: [loyalkaspar, SYFY Wizarding World](https://loyalkaspar.squarespace.com/syfy-wizarding-world). Trollbäck's later USA rebrand page names no typeface. Source: [Trollbäck, USA](https://trollback.com/projects/usa).
5. No Fonts In Use entry exists for USA Network; the ITC Avant Garde Gothic uses page lists no NBCU use. Source: [Fonts In Use, ITC Avant Garde Gothic](https://fontsinuse.com/typefaces/1143/itc-avant-garde-gothic).
6. Screenshots (DAMNATION, MR. ROBOT heroes; SAM RILEY'S BODY player): titles are wide geometric caps with a vertical-sided M, straight-leg R and circular O. ITC Avant Garde's M splays and its A apex is sharp; these are not. The caps match the Gotham / Core Sans A / Proxima class, consistent with finding 3. The player's credits ticker is the same family in bold.

### Shared UI sans

Both apps' nav, loglines, row labels and `E<n>` labels are the same face at the same sizes, which points to one NBCU app template skinned per brand (as the glossary's "Brand" entry assumes). No press or foundry source names it. Visual reading: low contrast, geometric skeleton with humanist detail, single-storey g, double-storey a, slanted-cut t, straight-tailed y. Closest commercial faces: Proxima Nova, Gotham, Core Sans A.

## Recommendations

| Role | Family (Google Fonts) | Weights to load | Styling | CSS fallback stack | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SYFY display | Barlow Condensed (Jeremy Tribby, 2017) | 700, 800 | uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.01em`, `line-height: 0.95` | `"Barlow Condensed", "Arial Narrow", "Roboto Condensed", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif; font-stretch: condensed` | OFL 1.1 |
| USA display | Montserrat (Ulanovsky et al., variable 100–900) | 600, 700 | uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.02em`, `line-height: 1.0` | `Montserrat, "Avenir Next", "Gill Sans", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` | OFL 1.1 |
| UI sans (both brands) | Figtree (Erik Kennedy, 2022, variable 300–900) | 300, 400, 600 | sentence case, `letter-spacing: 0` | `Figtree, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, Roboto, sans-serif` | OFL 1.1 |

Why these:

- **Barlow Condensed** is the closest Google face to Hero's proportions and weight at TV sizes, ships 18 static styles with good hinting, and was already on Google Fonts in October 2017 (period-correct). Its terminals are slightly rounded where Hero's are cut at 45°; accept that. Alternatives considered: Saira Condensed 700 (squarer bowls, closer in spirit, weaker hinting), Oswald 700 (too tall and traditional), Big Shoulders Display (angular cuts like Hero but too idiosyncratic).
- **Montserrat** at 600–700 in caps is the standard open stand-in for Gotham-class titling and matches the screenshots' width and M/R construction. URW Gothic (the open Avant Garde) is not on Google Fonts and the Avant Garde reading is not supported by the screenshots. Alternatives: Outfit, Jost (both narrower in caps).
- **Figtree** is designed as a Proxima Nova-class geometric-humanist sans with the same single-storey g and generous x-height, has a variable weight axis covering the 300/400/600 the UI needs, and pairs with both display faces without a second geometric voice clashing. Alternative: Inter (safer at 10-foot distances thanks to its opsz axis, but visibly cooler and wider than the original).

Licence notes (all three OFL 1.1, verified in each family's `METADATA.pb` in google/fonts: [Barlow Condensed](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/barlowcondensed/METADATA.pb), [Montserrat](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/montserrat/METADATA.pb), [Figtree](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/figtree/METADATA.pb)):

- Free to bundle in the app, embed in the web build and subset; no attribution required in the UI, but ship each family's `OFL.txt` alongside the font files.
- Modified builds must drop the Reserved Font Name; subsetting with the name intact is fine.
- Fonts may not be sold on their own; bundling with the app is fine.
- Self-host woff2 in the repo rather than loading from `fonts.googleapis.com`; Fire TV web views should not depend on a third-party CDN at launch, and self-hosting keeps the licence terms identical.

Implementation defaults decided here: load the three families as `@font-face` with `font-display: swap`; set `--font-display` per brand (`"Barlow Condensed"` for SYFY, `Montserrat` for USA) and `--font-ui: Figtree` globally; titles are `text-transform: uppercase` in CSS, not in the catalogue data.

## Open questions

1. The exact USA display cut (Gotham vs Core Sans A vs Proxima Nova) is unverified. A Brand New / UnderConsideration write-up of USA was not found; UnderConsideration's only USA Network page is a 2005 Speak Up thread. If an NBCU app style guide from 2017 surfaces, check it first.
2. Whether the NBCU app UI face is the same across NBC, Bravo, E! and Oxygen apps of the period would confirm the shared-template reading. Not needed for the rebuild.
3. GT Cinetype's role in SYFY (captions? on-air lower thirds?) is unconfirmed. If the Player's captions want a SYFY-specific voice later, a curve-free open face (e.g. Saira) could stand in; out of scope for #4.
4. Design Week (26 June 2017) and Creative Bloq coverage of the SYFY faces could not be fetched (bot blocks); the search excerpt of Design Week says the custom faces were used in app text. Treat as unverified.
